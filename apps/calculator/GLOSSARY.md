# Carbon-Footprint Calculation

Calculator collects Project and Participant activity data to calculate carbon footprints. Shared terms are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Language

**Carbon Footprint**:
The greenhouse-gas emissions attributed to a Project and its Participants, expressed as carbon-dioxide equivalent.
_Avoid_: CO₂ consumption

**Questionnaire Submission**:
The answers supplied for one Project Participation to calculate that Participant's contribution to the Carbon Footprint.
_Avoid_: Cost Submission

**Participant Travel Leg**:
A Participant's journey segment used in the carbon-footprint calculation.
_Avoid_: Ticket, Travel Cost Entry

**Project Shared Travel Leg**:
A journey segment owned by the Project and counted once in its carbon footprint rather than once per Participant.
_Avoid_: Project Activity

**Transport Emission Profile**:
A configured transport choice and its carbon-footprint calculation behavior.
_Avoid_: Cost category
