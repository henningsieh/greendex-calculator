---
title: Cost Tracker claims and settlement
description: Submit, review, and settle reimbursement claims in Cost Tracker.
---

## Prepare and submit a claim

As a Partner-side Project Coordinator, select a Payout Account, then create the Claim. Opening the workspace does not create a Claim. Complete the submission checklist, including costs, proof documents, and participant journeys, then submit.

Submission locks Partner edits. If Hosting staff return the Claim for correction, read their reason, make the changes, and submit again.

## Review and record payment

Hosting staff can request a correction, approve the calculated amount, or reject the Claim. Give a reason when requesting a correction or rejecting a Claim. Reopening a rejection returns the Claim to Hosting review without unlocking Partner edits.

Approval records the amount owed. It does not send money. Confirm payment in Cost Tracker only after transferring the full approved amount in one bank transfer. If you recorded payment by mistake, correct the paid flag and give a reason. This returns the Claim to Approved without reversing a bank transfer.

## Archive or reopen a Project

Settlement is open if any Project Partnership lacks a Claim or has a Claim that is neither paid nor rejected. An approved Claim awaiting payment still blocks archive. A Project with no Partnerships has nothing to settle and is ready to archive.

A Hosting Organization Owner, Admin, or assigned Hosting-side Project Coordinator can archive a ready Project after confirming the action. Archived records remain readable to users with access. Project work becomes read-only in Cost Tracker, and existing setup links close.

Only a Hosting Organization Owner or Admin can reopen the Project, and they must give a reason. Reopening the Project leaves all Claims unchanged and does not reactivate setup links. Reopening a rejected Claim is a separate action.
